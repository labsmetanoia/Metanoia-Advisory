# Website and platform audit — November 2026

Scope: every page of the production build (59 pages, both languages), the navigation and interactive components, performance, accessibility, SEO, security headers, reliability and the business workflows behind the site. Measured on the build at commit `76a1276` before changes and re-measured after. Findings are ordered by business impact.

## 1. Findings and what was done

| # | Area | Finding | Severity | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Lead capture | The contact form, deck-review form and newsletter form on the live site did not work. No form backend was configured, so the contact page showed a disabled placeholder with "email us" and the newsletter form posted to an empty HubSpot address and failed silently | Critical | A provider-agnostic form pipeline (Netlify Forms by default, Formspree or HubSpot by configuration). Every form is live without any key. Thank-you pages in both languages for the no-script path. Each submission carries page, referrer, campaign values, language and the service the visitor came from. Deck review accepts a file up to 10 MB |
| 2 | Deployment | Production deploys were paused by exhausted Netlify credits; four commits had not reached the live site | Critical (external) | Build stamp at `/version.txt` and in a `<meta name="build">` tag to verify deploys; GitHub Actions now builds and checks every push and keeps the built site as an artifact; a hosting-move procedure is documented. The credit decision stays with the owner |
| 3 | Credentials | The About page promised an eight-page credentials document that did not exist | High | Generated from the site's own content in both languages (`scripts/credentials-pdf.mjs`, 8 pages, under 1 MB each), offered behind a short email form so each download becomes a lead |
| 4 | Security headers | The Content-Security-Policy allowed inline scripts because the site configuration was injected as an inline script | Medium | Configuration moved to a `data-site` attribute; `'unsafe-inline'` removed from `script-src`. HSTS, nosniff, frame-ancestors and referrer policy were already in place. `/.well-known/security.txt` added |
| 5 | Performance | Stylesheet and script were shipped unminified (172 KB and 28 KB); hero slide images were 1,200 px wide for a 240 px slot on phones; the hero background was not preloaded; 1.6 MB of unused video and two unused font files were deployed | Medium | Build-time minification (146 KB and 20 KB before compression, roughly 25 KB and 7 KB over the wire); 600 px slide variants with `srcset`; hero image preload on the home page; dead assets removed |
| 6 | Language negotiation | The root redirect rules were shadowed by the root chooser page, so crawlers and visitors with scripts off saw the chooser instead of being sent to their language | Medium | Rules changed to forced redirects (`302!`); the chooser page remains as fallback and carries the current lockup |
| 7 | HTML validity | One placeholder form without a submit control; no other errors across 59 pages (checked with html-validate, stylistic rules off) | Low | Resolved by the form rewrite; validation added to `npm test` and to CI |
| 8 | Accessibility | Zero axe-core issues on the twelve tested templates. One heading-order warning on the Insights page (h3 after h1 when no articles are published) | Low | Heading fixed; the accessibility script's page list refreshed |
| 9 | Icons and manifest | No `favicon.ico` (browsers request it and got a 404) and no web app manifest | Low | Both added, generated from the monogram seal |
| 10 | SEO | All 59 pages have unique titles and descriptions, canonical and hreflang tags with x-default, Open Graph images, one h1, valid structured data. Eight titles exceed 60 characters because they carry the practice positioning; two legal descriptions are short | Low | Left as is (the long titles are deliberate positioning). FAQPage structured data added on the Contact and Strategic Storytelling pages |
| 11 | Runtime | No console errors, no failed requests and no horizontal overflow on any page at 320, 360, 390, 414, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1920 px | None | — |
| 12 | Links | No broken internal links across 5,600 checked; bilingual parity 100% | None | — |

## 2. Measurements

Lighthouse 12, run against the local build without HTTP compression (the live host compresses, so wire sizes and first paint are better than shown here). Scores are 0–100.

| Page | Device | Performance | Accessibility | Best practices | SEO | Largest paint | Layout shift |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home (before) | Phone | 72 | 97 | 100 | 100 | 6.0 s | 0 |
| Home (after) | Phone | 76 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 5.1 s | 0 |
| Home (after) | Desktop | 97 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 1.2 s | 0 |
| Contact (after) | Phone | 87 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 3.8 s | 0 |
| Contact (after) | Desktop | 100 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 0.8 s | 0 |
| Board & executive decks (after) | Phone | 80 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 4.7 s | 0.002 |

The phone score is held back by the full-bleed hero photograph and the 146 KB stylesheet, which is render-blocking. The stylesheet carries every revision's rules; Lighthouse estimates 85 KB of it unused on the home page. Splitting it per template or purging unused rules is the next performance step, listed below.

Other measurements: 59 pages validate; 5,621 internal links resolve; axe-core zero issues on twelve templates; no overflow at eight viewport widths; the credentials PDFs are 903 KB (EN) and 876 KB (ID).

## 3. Features added

| Feature | For whom | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Working forms with context | Clients and owner | Contact, deck review (with file upload), newsletter and credentials request all deliver to an inbox with page, referrer, campaign and service context; thank-you pages; three providers supported |
| Service finder | Clients | Three questions on the Strategic Storytelling and Contact pages recommend the closest service and a second option, and pre-fill the contact form with the chosen service |
| FAQ with structured data | Clients and search | Six questions before writing to the firm and five about the storytelling practice, answered from the firm's own published statements; FAQPage schema for rich results |
| Credentials document | Clients and owner | Eight-page bilingual PDF generated from the site content, offered behind an email form on the About page and linked from the footer |
| Lead triage automation | Owner | A serverless function classifies each submission with Claude (practice, likely service, urgency, fit, flags such as "listed company: conflict check") and drafts a first reply, delivered to Slack, Teams or email. Key-gated, optional |
| Instant deck diagnostic | Clients and owner | A visitor pastes a storyline and receives three observations and the page to rebuild first, in the shape of the human review; the email and summary reach the inbox so the human review can follow. Switched on by one flag once the API key is set |
| Cookieless analytics option | Owner | Plausible support alongside GA4, with the same event vocabulary (calls to action, forms, finder, downloads, diagnostic); campaign values captured into forms |
| Continuous checks | Owner | GitHub Actions builds, validates and keeps every push; `npm test` now includes HTML validation |
| Build stamp | Owner | `/version.txt` and a build meta tag to confirm what is live |

## 4. Recommended next steps (not done, in priority order)

1. **Resolve hosting credits** (owner decision): add Netlify credits, wait for the cycle, or move to GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages with the Formspree provider. Nothing else on this list reaches visitors until deploys resume.
2. **Set the keys** that switch on the optional automation: `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (triage and diagnostic), a Slack webhook or Resend key (lead delivery), a Plausible domain or GA4 ID (analytics), and a Calendly or Cal.com link in `calendarUrl` so "pick a time" books a real slot.
3. **Stylesheet weight**: purge rules unused on each template or split the phone-only revisions into a second file loaded by media query; expected to lift the phone performance score into the high 80s.
4. **Insights content**: the Insights section has no published articles; the first flagship note is the single strongest search and credibility asset still missing.
5. **Legal review**: the privacy, terms and conflicts pages remain drafts pending counsel sign-off (unchanged since launch).
6. **Mailbox**: `hello@metanoiaadvisors.com` has no MX records, so mail to it bounces until a mailbox is created.
